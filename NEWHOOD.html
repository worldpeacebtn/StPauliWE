Welcome to the Interactive Storyteller & IRL Reality Grid! This application blends digital cyberpunk web-apps with real-world pen-and-paper roleplay across St. Pauli and cosmic sectors.

Key Features:

  • Meshed 3D Stages: Toggle between Cubic courtroom and Spheric star grids in Three.js.
  • Case Vault & Universal Media: Bind and preview MP4, M4V, GLB 3D models, PDFs, HTML links, transparent PNGs, GIFs, and GitHub repos.
  • Character JRPG Editor: Customize stats, avatars, clans, and roles. Fully swappable Tribunal Stage roles!
  • Cyber-Scroll Viewer: Read long-form lore and documents like an ancient glowing cyber-scroll (📜).
  • Explorer vs Editor Mode: Switch effortlessly between playing the interactive story or editing the underlying vaults and scenes.
